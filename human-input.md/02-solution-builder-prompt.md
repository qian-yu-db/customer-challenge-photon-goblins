Banker retention and cross-sell radar

Protagonist Meridian Bank — Yusuf Demirel, EVP Consumer & Small Business Banking. He owns attrition reduction and cross-sell uplift across the branch network, where every point of attrition avoided is worth about $1.1M a year and missed next-best-action revenue leaves roughly $15M annually on the table.

Catalyst Customers are quietly slipping away: direct deposits stop, balances drain over several weeks, and the bank only notices after the relationship weakens. At the same time, relationship managers work from overnight extracts, so qualified customers are often never shown the right product offer during the call.

Journey Yusuf opens a live customer 360 and sees both signals in one place — balance runoff, payroll interruption, relationship depth, and eligibility for the next best product — sliced by segment and branch. He can ask which accounts are drifting, which customers are ready for an offer, and why the recommendation is being made, while the AI layer is governed end to end with Unity Catalog and the bank's approved semantic metrics. The same governed view keeps PII exposure tightly scoped to each banker’s role.

Resolution During the call, the banker uses the Databricks App to draft a retention save-offer and a cross-sell recommendation, then confirms the action so the system writes the follow-up and audit trail back to the operational store. Faster interventions recover at-risk customers, lift product penetration, and reduce avoidable attrition while keeping every model call, cost limit, and access decision auditable.
